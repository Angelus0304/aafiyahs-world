# Beat Maker Design Notes

## Purpose
The beat maker is a creative tool available in Aafiyah's World for composing loops, recording voice snippets, and shaping the sonic personality of the game world.

## Core Features
- Drum pad sequencer with kick, snare, hi-hat, and percussion layers
- Melody track with synth or piano-inspired tones
- Voice recording mode for Aafiyah's raw vocal samples
- Layer mixing controls for rhythm, ambience, and vocal balance
- Export support for saving loops to the player's creative library

## Gameplay Use
The beat maker can be used:
- as a side activity in the open world
- as a reward for collecting letters and words
- to trigger evolving transformations or unlock secret encounters
- to personalize the player's companion or boss battle soundtrack

## Mood and Style
The sound design should move between:
- cheerful, cartoon-like loops for the early companion phase
- haunting, compressed drum textures during transformation
- distorted, cinematic layers in the final horror phase

This makes the music a direct mirror of the creature's evolution.

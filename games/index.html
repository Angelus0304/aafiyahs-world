Aafiyah's World

The first character eats letters and forms words, evolving as the words become stronger. The transformation shifts the creature from a playful companion into the sinister Smiling Entity.

Game Pillars

  • Letter collection and word evolution
  • Companion-to-boss transformation
  • Open-world grassland visual identity
  • Beat maker and voice recording tools

Core Creative Loop

Aafiyah explores a bright world of scattered letters and hidden collectibles. Each word she forms fuels the creature’s next stage of evolution until it becomes the eerie final form seen in the horror art.

Game Concept

Aafiyah's World

A playful evolution game where a cheerful blocky companion eats letters, creates words, and transforms into a frightening smiling entity as the world shifts from joy into psychological horror.

Asset 1

The Blocky Companion

  • 3D model / overworld entity
  • Dark blocky humanoid form with glowing green eyes
  • Large yellow smiley head floating above the body
  • Bright grassy field with sunlight and low-poly scenery
  • Functions as a companion, mount, or hidden collectible
Asset 2

The Smiling Entity

  • 2D cinematic key art / horror boss design
  • Hollow eyes, wide grin, exposed teeth, and uncanny flesh tones
  • Typography reads “IT’S ME IT’S VERITY”
  • Used for title art, loading screens, and boss encounters
  • Represents the final evolution state of the creature
Built-in beat maker: Aafiyah can create beats, layer voice recordings, and shape the soundtrack of her world.
